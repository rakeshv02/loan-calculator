Frequently Asked Questions

How is my monthly payment calculated?
Using the standard amortisation formula: P × r(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1) where P=principal, r=monthly rate, n=number of payments.
Does this include fees?
No — enter the APR if you want to include fees in the rate.
